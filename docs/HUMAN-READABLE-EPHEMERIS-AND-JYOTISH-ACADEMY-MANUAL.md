### 3.1 The Classical *Trairashika* (Rule of Three) Formula for Planetary Longitude at Birth

Because the daily ephemeris tabulates planetary positions at `05:30 IST` (`00:00 UTC`), an astrologer finds the exact longitude (*Spashta Graha*) at any birth time $T_{\text{birth}}$ using the classical proportional formula:

$$\Delta t_{\text{hours}} = T_{\text{birth (IST)}} - 5.50\text{ hours}, \qquad f = \frac{\Delta t_{\text{hours}}}{24.0} = \frac{\text{Elapsed Ghatis}}{60.0}$$

$$\lambda_{\text{birth}} = \lambda_{05:30} + f \times (\text{Daily Motion } v_{\text{day}})$$

*Note:* If a planet is Retrograde (`R` / *Vakri*, where $v_{\text{day}} < 0$), subtract the proportional motion from $\lambda_{05:30}$. Rahu's mean motion (`-00° 03' 11"/day`) is always subtracted.

### 5.1 Manual *Vimshottari Dasha* (120-Year Cycle) Formula & Ready-Reckoner

**How to Compute the Birth Dasha Balance by Hand:**
1. Look up the **Moon's Sidereal Longitude ($\lambda_{\text{Moon}}$)** at birth.
2. Identify the Moon's **Nakshatra (`1..27`)** and its starting boundary (`Nakshatra span = 13° 20' = 800 arcminutes`).
3. Find the **remaining arcminutes (*Bhogyamsha*)** yet to be traversed in that Nakshatra: $B_{\text{arcmin}} = 800' - \text{elapsed arcminutes}$.
4. Multiply by the Mahadasha Lord's total years $Y_{\text{lord}}$ and divide by $800'$:

$$\text{Remaining Dasha Balance (Years)} = \frac{B_{\text{arcmin}}}{800'} \times Y_{\text{lord}}$$
